# Flashback

Flashback is a new way to share memories, designed to fetch pictures from multiple sources such as social media, phone storage, and/or cloud platforms. It helps users rediscover their past in a meaningful way by tapping daily into different vaults of pictures and bringing them back to the users.

Using *nostalgia-driven engagement*, Flashback leverages the emotional power of past photos, encouraging users to share their stories and integrate the app into their daily routine.

With a *social & personal timeline*, Flashback is not just a photo-sharing app: it’s a personal time capsule that connects people through memories.

Flashback encourages *fun and creative reinterpretations of old memories* using the camera, photo albums based on date and/or location, and nostalgic filters. It helps build a *social platform centered around storytelling* rather than instant, fleeting content.

*Flashback’s mission* is to provide users with a meaningful way to relive and share their personal history, fostering deeper connections through past experiences.

## Main Features

- ***Daily Memory Drop*** : Users receive a random photo from their past (or one selected by AI) with a prompt to reshare, reflect, or recreate.
- ***Memory Recreation*** : Users can take a side by side photo recreating their past moments.
- ***Social Feed & Engagement*** : Friends can like, comment, and tag people in their throwbacks.
- ***Memory Vault*** : A private collection of resurfaced memories (Pro feature).
- ***AI-Powered Throwback Suggestions*** : Smart AI curates meaningful moments from old photos.
